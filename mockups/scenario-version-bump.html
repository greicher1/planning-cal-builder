A new scenario and the Version

Two ways to offer the next version number when you press + Scenario. Each one only asks while Show info's Version box holds a number. Clear the box below to see that nothing is asked. Tab names stay Scenario A, B, C, and the band above the tabs gets a Version tile.

Try 3, 3.1, or leave it blank

A · Dialog when you press +

Asks before the copy is made. Enter takes the next version, Esc cancels the copy.

Press + Scenario. Copy a few times to see the number keep climbing past every tab.

B · One-click offer in the tab strip

The copy is made at once. The offer sits beside the tabs; ignoring it keeps the same version, and it goes away on your next edit or tab switch.

Press + Scenario, then pick one, or press Make an edit to see the offer step aside.

  • The number offered is the highest version on any tab, plus one, as a whole number: v3 or v3.1 both offer v4.
  • A plain text version saved before 18 Nov ("Blue") is never offered a number. The copy keeps it as it is.
  • The bump is part of making the copy, not an undo step of its own, and nothing new is saved in the file.
