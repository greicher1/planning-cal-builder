The note editor: cleaner looks

Today's editor next to three overhauls of it. Each holds exactly the same settings, nothing added or taken away, and each look is drawn for every kind of note editor, the waterfall's and the month view's. Everything is live: type, pick a colour, change Stays with, flip the switch.

Which editor:

TodayAs it is

Seven parts stacked. Small grey labels, a sentence of hint under Stays with, 18 colours always open, and a line of keyboard help.

AOne tidy list

The same parts as one aligned list: the name on the left, the value on the right, a hairline between. Colour folds into one row that opens the palette. A Save button.

A2A, reordered

Look A in the order you asked for: Day first, then Stays with, then every colour out as chips (one click, as today), then the view switch. The month view's editors have no Day, so they start at Stays with.

BText first, chips

The note's text is the editor. Its settings are a row of chips under it, each one showing its value and opening its own short menu. The smallest by far.

CEvery choice a button

Nothing hidden in a dropdown: Stays with as two buttons, the day as seven, ten colours in a row with + for the other eight. Cancel and Save. The clearest, and the tallest.

Picked: A2 (owner, 4 Nov 2026), with the automatic note's editor titled Auto-Note. A2 is A with your order: the day, then what it stays with, then the colour, then where it shows. Keeping every colour out makes the colour one click again, as it is today, for about 50 px more height than A.

My first pick was A. It changes the least about how the editor works (every control is where today's is, in the same order), and it is the one that reads cleanly at a glance: one column of names, one column of values. B is the neatest but hides each choice one click away. C is the easiest for someone new but grows to about the height of the grid's visible weeks. Whatever you pick, both note editors get it, the waterfall's and the month view's, and so do the month view's holiday and automatic-note editors.
