Production: work back from the wrap

Mock-up, not the app. Production's date can be when the shoot starts or when it wraps. Pick Ends and the wrap holds: change an episode's days or add a hiatus, and the start moves instead.

Try it: on the Production card, switch Starts to Ends (the ringed word). Click the date and pick Fri 4/9/27. Then give an episode 6 days, or turn on Hiatus, and watch the start move while the wrap stays. Switch back to Starts and do the same to compare.

What changes

The Starts word over Production's date becomes a choice, Starts or Ends, as on the Weeks phases.

In Ends the date is the wrap, the last shoot day. The start is counted back from the shoot days, skipping weekends, holidays, hiatus days and days marked Off. A line under the date says where the start landed.

Ruled 30 Oct

The shoot wraps exactly on the day you pick, so the start can fall mid-week.

The picker offers only shoot days. A typed weekend or holiday reads as the shoot day before it.

  2. The shift tools (Shift All, Shift From, Anchor To, Rebuild From) keep moving the shoot as they do now, and wherever it then wraps becomes the held wrap? Suggested: yes.
  3. ↳ Start after Prod Prep is hidden while Ends is picked (shown here), as on the Weeks phases. Or keep it, and have it switch Production back to Starts?

Shown, not asked: switching to Ends fills in the current wrap, so nothing moves. Snap to Monday is hidden in Ends. Switching back to Starts keeps the start where it is, and turns Snap to Monday off if that start isn't a Monday. Prep moves with the start. The worked-out start is still saved, so an older version opens the calendar as a shoot that starts on that day.

About this mock-up (not part of the design)

  • A made-up calendar: six episodes of 5 days each, 30 shoot days. Prod Prep is fixed at 1/11/27 to 2/19/27.
  • One holiday, Good Friday 3/26/27. Production's own hiatus is fixed at 3/15/27 for 1 week. No days are marked Off or half.
  • The grid mixes the waterfall's week rows with the month view's days, so the exact start and wrap are visible. It is not either view.
  • The ringed parts, the "holds" tags beside the grid and the "What moved" strip are mock-up markings. Simultaneous Post is left out.
