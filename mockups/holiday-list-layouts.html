Holidays list: simpler layouts

The live list (first card) repeats headings when phases overlap or a break interrupts one, and the "also …" lines add noise. Four calmer ways to lay out the same holidays. None of them repeat a heading or say "also …"; each holiday names one phase at most, in plain grey text on its date line. The calendar is made up: Rigging is a custom phase counted in Days that overlaps Post.
