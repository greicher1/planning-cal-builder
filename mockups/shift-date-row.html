Shift: the phase beside the date

Today, under All phases + To a date, the phase you pick sits between What moves and How far, although it is the subject of the date row: your eye goes up to the phase, back down past the How far toggle, and has to work out that it means "Shoot starts on 3/2/26". Proposed: the date row carries its own subject. Under All phases (or your own ticks) the phase select sits under the How far toggle, directly above "starts on"; under Phase & before / after the phase stays in What moves, where it is the pivot, and the date row repeats it as plain text, since it is pinned anyway. By weeks does not change.

Both boxes are live and mirror each other: click in either. The numbered dots mark the reading order for the case you picked.
