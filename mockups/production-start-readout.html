About this mock-up (not part of the design)

  • The two cards are the same Production card, one on Starts and one on Ends, so you can compare them. Each card's caption switches it.
  • Only the date, the span and the totals box are drawn. The episode list, Hiatus and Simultaneous Post sit below as they do now.
  • The made-up calendar: six episodes of 5 days, Good Friday 3/26/27, and a 1-week hiatus at 3/15/27 when it's on. The Starts card starts Mon 2/22/27; the Ends card wraps Fri 4/9/27.
  • The amber flash marks what moved. It is a mock-up marking.
