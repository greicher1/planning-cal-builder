Link a note to a phase

A note you type stays on its week, as it does today, or stays with a phase's week and moves wherever that phase goes. That covers typing a start date, the month drag, Rebuild From, Close all gaps, Shift, and a hiatus added before it. One new row in the note editor; the week stays the default. R1, the words:

The waterfall as it stands, Production starting 5/2/33. Click any Notes cell, an empty one too. The amber dot marks the new row; everything else in the editor is today's. Tab or Ctrl/Cmd-Enter saves, as does clicking away; Esc cancels.

What changes

  • One row, under the text: the week (the default) or a phase drawn in that row, by the cell's own label.
  • A hint under it says what will happen.
  • No row in a week where no phase runs (try 6/27/33).
