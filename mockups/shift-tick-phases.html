Shift: tick the phases that move, four ways

In every layout a list of the phases carries a tick each, and shortcuts fill the ticks in: All phases ticks every phase, Phase & before and Phase & after tick the phase you pick and every phase that starts before or after it. Click any tick to change the set; a set that matches no shortcut leaves no shortcut pressed. Each row says where that phase goes. The four tabs differ only in how the phase is picked, whether By weeks / To a date is a switch, and how much shows before you ask.

Try your case in each: Phase & after on Pre-Prep. Writer's Rm runs into Pre-Prep but started earlier, so it stays unticked. The calendar is made up.

The four layouts

  • A: three buttons, the phase dropdown, the list, a By weeks / To a date switch. Every step labelled; the same parts as Rebuild From.
  • B: as A without the dropdown. The list picks the phase: every row has a pin, and the pinned row is the one the buttons and the date work from.
  • C: as A without the switch. The weeks row and the date row are both always there, each with its own buttons.
  • D: the fewest controls. The list is always open, the three shortcuts are small links in its header, the pin picks the phase, both rows show.

In every layout

  • It opens on All phases, so typing 2 and pressing Later does what it always did.
  • To a date pins the phase that lands on your date: it has to move, so its tick is fixed.
  • Whole schedule (All phases, To a date) ticks every phase and fixes them.
  • Ticks are forgotten when the box closes, as Rebuild From's are.
  • The engine already takes any set of phases, so each is panel work only.

Your calls

  • T0, the layout: A, B, C or D. (A recommended: nothing to learn, and it is Rebuild From's parts in Rebuild From's order. B if you would change Rebuild From the same way.)
  • T1, the list under All phases (A, B, C): folded behind "Choose phases…" until you open it, or always shown as in D?
  • T2, notes for your own set: a note moves when its week is inside a ticked phase's run, even a week an unticked phase shares (recommended), or only weeks no unticked phase runs in?
