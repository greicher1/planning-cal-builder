Shift: tick the phases that move

The three buttons stay, as shortcuts. Each one fills in the ticks: All phases ticks every phase, Phase & before and Phase & after tick the phase you pick and every phase that starts before or after it. Then click any tick to change the set. A set that matches no shortcut leaves none of the three pressed. Each row says where that phase goes.

Try your case: pick Phase & after and Pre-Prep. Writer's Rm runs into Pre-Prep but started earlier, so it stays unticked. Or tick Writer's Rm too, or untick Localization, and press Later. The calendar is made up.

What's new

  • A list of the phases under the phase, with a tick each and where it lands (Moves / Stays by weeks, the new date and Pushed / Pulled to a date). It replaces the line that named what stays.
  • The three buttons fill in the ticks. Ticks you change yourself make your own set, and no button shows pressed.
  • To a date: the phase that lands on your date is pinned (it must move), as Rebuild From pins its phase.

As shown

  • It still opens on All phases, By weeks, so typing 2 and pressing Later does what it always did.
  • Whole schedule (All phases, To a date) ticks every phase and locks them.
  • Ticks are forgotten when the box closes, as Rebuild From's are.
  • The engine already takes any set of phases (live since 5 Nov 2026), so this is the panel only.
