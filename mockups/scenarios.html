What you're looking at

One file, three scenariosThe same calendar three ways: 10 episodes, 8 episodes starting two weeks later, and 10 episodes shot in Toronto. Each tab is a whole calendar, drawn by the real app.

How it behaves

  • One .sptcal holds every scenario. With one, the file is exactly today's.
  • A tab is a whole calendar. Nothing is shared between tabs, so nothing changes behind your back.
  • Flipping keeps your place: the same view, the same month, the same scroll, so anything that moved shifts in front of you. Ctrl + Page Down / Page Up flips, as in Excel.
  • Letters, not numbers. A scenario is an option, not a version. The header's v3 is each tab's own revision number, as today.
  • Exports are the tab you're on, and their names say which one.
  • Each tab has its own undo. Switching is not an edit.
  • Up to 50 tabs. They scroll sideways when they don't fit. Compare shows six at a time, and with more you tick which. Nothing appears until you add a second.
