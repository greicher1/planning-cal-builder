The 21 it came from

My pick: 21, “Four labelled families”

Today’s toolbar is already close; what makes it slow to read is that its four families of tools are told apart only by gaps, the view toggle is a different height from everything else, and the six text buttons read as six separate things. 21 fixes exactly those and nothing more:
  • Hairline dividers between the families (view, text, dates, page, history), so the eye groups them without reading.
  • The text buttons joined into one strip, like every word processor’s formatting bar, so they read as one tool.
  • Small labels over the three tool families (Text, Move dates, Page), the only words added, for anyone new.
  • Everything one height (the view toggle shrinks from 34 to 30 px to match), and Undo / Redo joined as a pair.
  • Keeps what already works: the navy outline that marks the date tools (your pick of 21 Oct), the text beside Shift, and every button where it is today.
