Weeks phases: count back from an end date

Mock-up, not the app. A Weeks phase's date can be when it starts or when it ends. Pick Ends and the end holds: change the weeks or add a hiatus, and the start moves instead.

Try it: on the Post card, switch Starts to Ends (the ringed word), then change 20 weeks to 22, or turn on Hiatus. Click the date to see the picker. Switch back to Starts and do the same to compare.

What changes

The Starts word over a Weeks phase's date becomes a choice: Starts or Ends, the same way Length · Weeks is a choice. Everything else on the card stays.

It is not Dates mode: Dates fixes both ends. An Ends phase still delivers every week, so a hiatus moves its start earlier.

Ruled 30 Oct

With Ends picked, the phase always ends on the Friday of the week you pick. There is no switch: Snap to Monday is hidden, as it is for Dates.

A typed Wednesday reads as that week's Friday, and the picker offers only Fridays.

About this mock-up (not part of the design)

  • A made-up calendar: Production runs 3/2/26 to 5/29/26, and Post is the only phase you can change.
  • Post always has its own column. The app would put it beside Production only where the two overlap.
  • The hiatus is Post's own, fixed at 8/3/26 for 2 weeks. There are no holidays.
  • The ringed word and the "holds" tags beside the grid are mock-up markings, and so is the "What moved" strip.
