Double-up days: joined to the end of the shoot pill

A double-up comes straight out of the end of its episode’s pill, on the same line. The next episode starts on the line below for the days they overlap. Drag a double-up onto another episode’s days and it lets go and becomes its own pill; it can also go after wrap. Drop it back on the end, or double-click it, and it joins up again. Its own episode’s days grey out while you drag, because a double-up there would just be the same episode twice. The main unit’s dates never move.
