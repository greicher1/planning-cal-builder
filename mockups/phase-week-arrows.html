Phase week arrows

Owner, 4 Nov 2026: “on the phases in the leftside column, above and below the date box, little up and down arrows appear allowing you to shift up or down by a week at a time. Waterfall only.” Plan: PHASE-WEEK-ARROWS-PLAN.md.

Try it: pick a layout, point at a phase card and click ▲ or ▼. Click Prod Prep's ▲ three times to see the waterfall gain a column. With the date box focused, Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ does the same. Click a card's chevron to fold it (try F). Switch the preview to Month and the arrows go.

What it does

  • ▲ moves the phase one week up the waterfall (earlier). ▼ moves it one week down (later).
  • Only that phase moves, so the gaps on each side of it change. Use the Shift tool to move several phases and keep the gaps between them.
  • Waterfall only: the arrows show while the waterfall is on screen. They change the calendar's own date, so the month view follows.
  • The toolbar's ← 1 wk / 1 wk → still move the whole calendar.

What moves with it, what stays

  • Moves: its cells, its automatic notes (Writer's Room Opens, Principal Photography), its own hiatus, and what is set on its cells (spans, column swaps, text styles). Production's Off and half days stay on the same shoot days.
  • Stays: every other phase, typed notes (Table read), holidays (Memorial Day), all-phase hiatuses, the calendar range.
  • Can change: how many columns the waterfall needs, if the phase now overlaps another.
