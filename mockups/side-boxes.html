Side boxes beside the waterfall

Mock-up for SIDE-BOXES-PLAN.md (owner ruling 7). One made-up calendar, three outputs: the screen, the waterfall PDF and the Excel workbook. Purple marks are new controls or annotations, not part of the output.

1. On screen (waterfall view)

  • Side boxes ▾ in the preview toolbar adds a box to either side (its menu is shown open below).
  • Left: a text box. The second line is being edited, with the header's format bar above it (bold is on).
  • Right: the colour key. Pre Prep and Prod Prep share a colour here, so they share one row. Hover a row for its × (hide), if Q2 is answered that way.
  • ⋮⋮ drags a box up, down or to the other side. The bar on each side's inner edge drags its width.

The Side boxes menu, open. It shows only in the Waterfall view (Q7). Each item adds the box at the bottom of that side and puts the cursor in its title.

2. Waterfall PDF

  • Grid and boxes are fitted to the page together (whole-percent scale, as today). The header spans both.
  • Without boxes this calendar prints portrait. With a box on each side it is too wide for portrait, and landscape prints more than 15% larger, so the existing orientation rule turns it (Q4).
  • Dashed purple outlines show the grid's own extent; they are not printed.

Today, no boxes: portrait, the grid alone.

With boxes: landscape, the grid printed beside them.

3. Excel

  • Each side is one column at the side's width, with an empty spacer column (hatched) between it and the grid. A left side moves the grid two columns right.
  • Boxes start on row 1. A title or a key entry is one row; a text line is merged over as many rows as it needs (the 14 pt line below takes two). One empty row between boxes.
  • The print area covers everything, and fit-to-page (already on) puts it on one page.
