Anchor To: choose what moves

Today Anchor To always moves every phase, so the one you pick lands on your date. A and B give it one more choice: move everything, or only that phase and the ones after it, or only that phase and the ones before it. C goes further and makes Shift From and Anchor To one tool. The gaps between the phases that move are kept in every case.

Try it: delivery is fixed and the shoot must start 2 weeks earlier. In A or B, pick Phase & before and press Go. In C, pick Phase & before (Production is already chosen under it), then type 2 and press Earlier, or switch to To a date. The calendar is made up.

What's new

  • One choice: All phases, Phase & before, Phase & after.
  • A line under it naming the phases that stay where they are.
  • A and B: Shift From gets the same choice, Phase & before or Phase & after, so you can move a phase and the ones before it by a number of weeks. Click Shift From to see it.
  • C only: a second choice, By weeks or To a date, so Shift From and Anchor To become one Shift tool and their buttons go.

As shown

  • A and B: Anchor To opens on All phases and Shift From on Phase & after, so both work as they do today until you change them. C opens on All phases, By weeks: today's Shift All form.
  • In C the phase sits right under the choice. It shows whenever a phase matters: for Phase & before or Phase & after, and for All phases to a date (the phase that lands on the date).
  • Whole schedule only goes with All phases.
  • Holidays, locked hiatuses and dated notes still never move. A phase's own notes and hiatuses move with it.
  • Nothing goes into the saved file.

Your calls

  • R2, on opening: back to All phases (and, in C, By weeks) every time, or remember your last choice until the app closes?
