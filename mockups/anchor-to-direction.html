Anchor To: choose what moves

Today Anchor To always moves every phase, so the one you pick lands on your date. Here the popover has one more choice under the phase: move everything, or only that phase and the ones after it, or only that phase and the ones before it. The gaps between the phases that move are kept either way.

Try it: delivery is fixed and the shoot must start 2 weeks earlier. Pick This and before and watch Post and Localization stay put below, then press Go. The calendar is made up.

What's new

  • One choice: Everything, This and after, This and before.
  • A line under it naming the phases that stay where they are.
  • The result line says how many moved and how many stayed.

As shown

  • It starts on Everything, so the tool works as it does today until you change it.
  • Whole schedule hides the choice: there's nothing before or after it.
  • Holidays, locked hiatuses and dated notes still never move. A phase's own notes and hiatuses move with it.
  • Nothing goes into the saved file.

Your calls

  • R1, layout: three buttons (A) or a dropdown that names the phase (B)?
  • R2, on opening: back to Everything every time, or remember your last choice until the app closes?
