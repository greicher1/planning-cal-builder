About this mock-up (not part of the design)

  • A is the card as built on the branch (the span under the date box, with "Start after" moved up). B and C are the two places you asked about. D and E are my two extra ideas, and F is D with its two sections swapped, as you asked: the span opens the box, the total comes after it.
  • D puts everything about how long the shoot runs in one box: the shoot days, then the dates, weeks and days lost, then the episodes those days are made of. The date box above stays for the input.
  • E moves the span into the card's coloured header, in place of the "80 shoot days → 17 wk · …" line. It's the only one you still see with the card folded (try "Fold the cards"). The shoot-day count isn't lost: it's in the Total Shooting Days box. The STARTS / WRAPS words go, since the pills sit right under the name.
  • C sits under the whole shooting order, so with ten episodes it's a long way from the date it depends on. Try "4 episodes" to see it on a short list.
  • The calendar is made up. Pre-Production, Hiatus and Simultaneous Post are drawn but do nothing here.
