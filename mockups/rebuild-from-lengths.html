Build Around (was Rebuild From): lengths in the box, both sides at once

Ruled 5 Nov 2026: layout B, and the tool is renamed Build Around. Production's length is read-only in the box. R2 (a) and R3 (yes) ruled too, and Build Around is built.

Rebuild From pins one phase to a date and lines the other phases up end to end around it, from their lengths. Today it can't place a phase until a length has been typed for it in the sidebar, and on a new calendar the box offers nothing at all. In A, B and C every row has a length box: type a number and the row ticks itself and shows where that phase would land. Nothing changes until you press Build, and one undo takes it all back, lengths included. (A and C carry the new name too, for comparison.)

Things to try: on New, Show info filled in, pick a shoot date, then type a length for Pre-Prep, Prod Prep and Post. On New, nothing entered, see what the box says while Production has no length. On In progress, change Prod Prep from 9 to 11: with the shoot pinned, the prep starts two weeks earlier; pin Prod Prep instead and the shoot and Post are pushed two weeks. On Today, see what the live box does to Writer's Rm, and what Close all gaps does to the shoot. The calendars are made up.

The layouts

  • Today: the live box, for comparison.
  • A: today's layout plus the lengths. Still one side at a time: Work backwards, or Work forwards.
  • B: one list for both sides, with the pinned phase in its place. Before · Both · After are shortcuts that tick a side, like Shift's. Opens on Both. Adds ends by.
  • C: as B with no dropdown. The list is the whole form: a pin on each row picks the phase, and the pinned row carries its own date.

In A, B and C

  • Writer's Rm starts unticked (your call). Tick it and it is rebuilt like any other phase.
  • Close all gaps is gone. It pushed the whole shoot on every one of your calendars.
  • Any phase can be pinned, with or without a length.
  • Typing a length ticks a row that had none.
  • Custom phases start unticked, as today.
  • Production's length can't be changed here (your call): it shows Show info's shoot days, read-only.
