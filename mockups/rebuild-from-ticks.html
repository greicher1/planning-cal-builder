Rebuild From: choose which phases move

Today Rebuild From moves every phase on one side of your date that has a length, and asks nothing. Here the popover lists those phases first, with a tick box each and the date each would get, so you can leave Localization out or bring a custom phase in before anything moves.

Try it: untick Localization, tick Casting, or change the date. The made-up calendar has Production pushed 2 weeks to 6/21/27.

What's new

  • A list of the phases on that side, in the order they'd be chained.
  • A tick each. Unticked phases stay where they are.
  • Each ticked phase shows its new start, and how far it moves, before you press Rebuild.

As shown

  • Built-in phases start ticked, custom phases unticked.
  • Ticks are forgotten when the popover closes, so nothing goes into the saved file.
  • Close all gaps is unchanged.

Your calls

  • Starting ticks: as shown, or also leave out any phase that already runs alongside another (Localization and Casting here)?
  • Close all gaps: leave it, or have it use the same ticks?
