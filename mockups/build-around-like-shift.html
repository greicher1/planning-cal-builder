Shift is on the left for reference, and today's Build Around on the right shows the same pin, date and ticks in today's layout.

All three are live on one made-up calendar. Try in the middle box: click Post to pin it, drag down a few rows, press After …, type a length, press Build.

New: which phases start unticked. A phase is left out only when it runs alongside the phase it would be built against, and a line under the buttons says so. Switch between the two calendars, then click Pre Prep and press Prior to Pre Prep on each.

What changed, against the list

  1. Header strip with a select-all box and the count, in place of the column header.
  2. "Phases" as the caption; the count moved into the strip ("4 of 6 build").
  3. A click on a row no longer flips its tick. The box ticks.
  4. Drag to tick or clear a run. Rows with no length are skipped.
  5. The outline on the chosen row, as in Shift.
  6. No hover tooltips on rows.
  7. The hint opens upward.
  8. "starts on", and the title reads "Build Around".
  9. The pin is the row you click; the dropdown under the list follows, and picks it too.
  10. The date row is under the list, the phase directly above "starts on".
  11. Prior to … / After … as buttons under the list, in place of the switch above it. Both is gone (your ruling): the box at the top of the list ticks every phase.

Different from Shift, on purpose

  • Ticking a box does not move the pin. In Shift the outline follows every tick; here the pin sets the date and the whole preview, so only a click on the row moves it.
  • The pinned row has a pin, not a tick. It is fixed to the date; there is nothing to untick.
  • The Length column, typed in the list as today.
  • The dark Build button. Its label says what will happen, and nothing changes until it is pressed.
  • How it opens: both sides ticked, less any phase that runs alongside its neighbour. That is a change from today, where Writer's Rm and custom phases always start unticked.
