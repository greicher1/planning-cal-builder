Double-up days: every UI piece

A Double-ups card in the Phases tab, like Episodic Prep’s: its name is the default pill name, its − / + is the default count, and its list gives each episode (or block) its own name and count. The month view draws the pills. Right-click a pill for Rename…, Put back and Remove; double-click one to jump to its row.
