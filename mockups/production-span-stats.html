Production: weeks, holidays and Off days on the shoot span

Mock-up, not the app. Five ways to show three counts with the Starts and Wraps pills: the shoot's weeks, the holidays that cost it a shoot day, and the Off days that cost it one. Hover any count to see what it's made of.

About this mock-up (not part of the design)

  • What counts. A holiday counts only on a weekday the shoot would otherwise have worked, so a weekend holiday, one inside a hiatus, one marked Off, or one you're working doesn't count. An Off day counts only on a weekday the shoot would otherwise have worked too. The app already keeps the holiday list (the Holidays list's "costs a shoot day" flag reads it). The Off-day count would follow the same rule.
  • Weeks is the same number as the "→ 22 wk" in the card's top line. If one of these goes in, that line could drop it.
  • A zero. A count of zero stays, in grey, so the three keep their places. Try "No Off days".
  • The made-up calendar: a 100-day shoot from Mon 1/11/27 on the U.S. Area Standards holidays, with Fri 2/12/27 and Mon 4/19/27 marked Off. The longer shoot is 145 days.
  • Only the date, the span and the top of the totals box are drawn. The rest of the card sits below as it does now.
