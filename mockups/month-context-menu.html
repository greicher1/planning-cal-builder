How it behaves

  • The menu is for the day under the pointer. “This day” is always first, so Add 1-day phase here and Add 1-week phase here never move. Below it is one section for each thing on the day: the bar you clicked, the shoot day, a hiatus, a holiday.
  • A choice the app refuses is greyed out with its reason, never hidden.
  • Shift + right-click still opens the browser’s own menu.
  • In the open menu: ↑ ↓ move, → opens a submenu, Enter chooses, Esc closes.
  • Each item is one undo step.
  • Left-click on a day’s number opens the same menu, under the number. It replaces the Full day/Off popover. Click the number again to close it. A click on a “+” slot or a note still opens the note editor, and a bar still drags.

Left out on purpose

  • Shift From, Anchor To, Rebuild From. Owner, 19 Oct 2026. They stay in the toolbar.
  • Marking a half day. Withdrawn on 15 Oct 2026. A half day is where an episode or block switches.
  • Renaming or recolouring a hiatus. Only the waterfall has that editor today.
  • Opening the menu from the keyboard. No day in the month view can take focus, and adding that would change the frozen renderer.
